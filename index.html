Buyer's Guide

Night Vision Quality


When choosing a pet camera, night vision quality is crucial. Look for cameras with features like color night vision for better clarity in low-light conditions.

Video Resolution


Aim for cameras with at least 1080p HD resolution to ensure clear image quality. This is especially important for identifying any behavior issues with your cat.

Subscription Requirements


Some cameras offer features that require a subscription. Understand what functionalities come with the device without additional fees, such as video storage or live streaming.

Field of View


A wider field of view allows you to monitor your cat more effectively. Models with pan and tilt capabilities can cover larger areas.

Interactive Features


Consider whether you want interactive features like treat tossing or two-way audio. These can enhance your experience and make it easier to engage with your pet.
